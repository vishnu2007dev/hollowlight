Hollowlight: a free browser arena survivor game

You're a lantern spirit holding back waves of shadow creatures until dawn. Move with WASD, the arrow keys or touch; your weapons fire on their own. Level up, pick upgrades, beat two bosses, chain kills for combos and grab pink wisps for big points. Choose Easy or Normal and a 5 or 10 minute run.

Free and open source: source code on GitHub.
